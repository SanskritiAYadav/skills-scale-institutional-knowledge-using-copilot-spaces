# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central index for OctoAcme project management guidance, roles, and delivery practices.

## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project management approach designed to move from idea to delivery with clarity and accountability. The team emphasizes:

- Clear ownership and roles across product, project, engineering, and QA work
- A lifecycle that moves through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective
- Regular communication through standups, planning sessions, and stakeholder updates
- Small, testable increments with explicit acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Risk management and escalation practices to keep work moving and issues visible
- Measured outcomes and continuous improvement based on delivery evidence and feedback

## Working model at a glance

1. Initiation: confirm the problem, stakeholders, goals, and decision to proceed
2. Planning: align scope, milestones, backlog, risks, and responsibilities
3. Execution: deliver in small increments with reviews, testing, and tracking
4. Release: validate quality, communicate changes, and support deployment
5. Close and improve: capture learnings and feed them back into the process
